How It Works

Sign up. Get placed on a team. Race.

01

We Build the Teams

Every athlete is placed onto a team balanced for a fair event matchup. Feel free to sign up solo or invite some friends to join in.

02

Race Roulette

Each event is pulled at random from the pool and revealed one at a time. Once it's announced, your team decides together who runs it. Nobody knows what gets pulled next.

03

One Event, Guaranteed

Every athlete runs exactly one individual event. No repeats, no sitting out, and you'll always have a say in which race becomes yours.

04

The Relay Finale

Four athletes from each team return for a relay finale to close out the day. It's also pulled at random from the pool.

05

Strategy is Key

A smarter team can beat a faster one, because at Velox 1, Faster Isn't Enough.
